FAQ

Common questions

How long has Kapri Logistics been in business?

Kapri Logistics was established in 1994 as a transport logistics firm and has since grown into a one-stop, end-to-end supply chain partner — bringing 30+ years of hands-on SCM and logistics experience.

What services does Kapri Logistics offer?

Contract Logistics & 3PL, Warehouse Management, Stores Management, Project Management and Transportation — delivered as one integrated, end-to-end supply chain solution. See our full services.

Does Kapri Logistics provide 3PL and warehouse management?

Yes — contract logistics and 3PL covering receiving, storage, preservation, picking & packaging and loading/unloading, plus warehouse management with long-term space outsourcing, multi-client facilities, and space-optimization consulting.

Where is Kapri Logistics located?

Our office is at 152, Centre Facility Building No 2, Opp. Jalaram Market, Sector 19, Vashi, Navi Mumbai - 400705, India. See the map and directions.

Was Kapri Logistics founded by a group of promoters?

No. Kapri Logistics was independently founded and built by its founder starting in 1994, later expanding from transportation into contract logistics, warehouse management and 3PL.
