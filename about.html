History

Established in 1994

Kapri Logistics was established in 1994 as an upcoming firm in transport logistics — independently founded and grown, not started by a group of promoters. From day one, the focus was simple: move goods reliably, and earn the right to do more.

That focus paid off. Three decades later, that same discipline underpins every warehouse we run and every route we plan today.

Background

A consistent ramp-up, vertical by vertical

With a consistent ramp-up plan, we've geared up with additional verticals — contract logistics and warehouse management — built deliberately on top of our original transportation strength, rather than chased opportunistically.

Today, that means clients get transportation, contract logistics & 3PL, and warehouse management from a single accountable partner, instead of stitching together multiple vendors.
